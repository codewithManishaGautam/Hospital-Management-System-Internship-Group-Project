Hospital Management System (HMS) Billing Module 

1. Introduction
The Billing Module in the Hospital Management System (HMS) is designed to manage all patient-related billing activities in an organized and automated manner.
This module integrates multiple hospital departments such as Laboratory, Pharmacy, Diagnostic, Insurance, Nursing, and Doctor Consultation into a centralized billing workflow.
The system allows department-wise bill generation while maintaining a unified billing dashboard for the billing department.
All generated billing PDFs are finally merged into a single document and sent to the patient via email.

2. Objective of the Billing Module
The main objectives of the billing module are:
To automate hospital billing operations 
To reduce manual billing errors 
To maintain department-wise financial records 
To generate and manage billing PDFs 
To provide centralized billing monitoring 
To improve patient billing transparency 
To send consolidated billing reports digitally

5. Detailed Workflow Explanation
5.1 Patient Admission Process (IPD)
When a patient is admitted to the hospital under the IPD section, the hospital staff creates a new patient entry in the HMS.
The following details are stored:
Patient ID 
Patient Name 
Age and Gender 
Admission Date 
Ward/Room Details 
Insurance Information 
Doctor Information 
After admission, a billing account is automatically created for the patient.

5.2 Daily Laboratory Billing Flow
Doctors may prescribe various laboratory tests during the patient’s stay.
Examples:
Blood Test 
Urine Test 
CBC Test 
Sugar Test 
Laboratory Billing Process
Doctor Prescribes Test
            ↓
Lab Test Performed
            ↓
Lab Bill Generated
            ↓
Lab PDF Created
            ↓
Payment Collected in Lab Department
            ↓
PDF Displayed on Billing Dashboard
Important Point
The payment for laboratory services is collected directly by the Laboratory Department, not by the central billing department.
However, the generated laboratory PDF is shared with the billing dashboard for record management.
5.3 Diagnostic Billing Flow

Diagnostic Billing Process
Diagnostic Test Requested
            ↓

            ↓
Diagnostic Charges Calculated
            ↓

            ↓

5.4 Pharmacy Billing Flow

8. PDF Merge Process
All PDFs generated from different departments are merged into a single consolidated PDF document.
PDFs Included in Merge

12. Conclusion
The HMS Billing Module provides a centralized and efficient billing management solution for hospitals.
Different departments independently manage their own charges and billing activities, while all generated billing documents are integrated into a centralized billing dashboard.
The final merged PDF system improves document organization and enhances the patient experience by providing a single comprehensive billing document via email.
