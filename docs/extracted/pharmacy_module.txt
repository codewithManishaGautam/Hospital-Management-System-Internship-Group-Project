Pharmacy Module – Hospital Management System (HMS)
1. Introduction
The Pharmacy Module is an important part of the Hospital Management System (HMS).
It helps manage the complete medicine billing and prescription process digitally inside the hospital.
This module connects:
Doctor Dashboard
Pharmacy Department
Billing Department
Insurance Department
When a doctor creates a prescription, it is automatically sent to the pharmacy dashboard.
The pharmacist checks the prescription, provides medicines, generates the bill, and sends the PDF bill to billing and insurance departments.
The system reduces manual paperwork, improves accuracy, and saves time.

2. Objective
The main objectives of the Pharmacy Module are:
Automate medicine billing process
Reduce manual prescription handling
Maintain medicine records digitally
Generate bills automatically
Improve communication between departments
Track OPD/IPD medicine billing
Send bill PDF to billing department
Maintain payment and insurance records
Reduce medicine calculation errors

5. Working Process
Step-by-Step Flow
Step 1 – Doctor Prescription
Doctor logs into Doctor Dashboard and writes prescription.
Step 2 – Auto Send
Prescription automatically sent to Pharmacy Module.
Step 3 – Pharmacist Review
Pharmacist opens prescription and checks medicines.
Step 4 – Medicine Entry
Medicines added with:
quantity
batch
expiry
price
Step 5 – Bill Generation
System calculates total automatically.
Step 6 – Payment
Patient payment processed:
Cash
Online
Insurance
Step 7 – PDF Generation
Bill PDF generated automatically.
Step 8 – Department Sharing
PDF sent to:
Billing Department
Insurance Department
Step 9 – Record Storage
All billing data stored in HMS database.
